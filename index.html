How to Order

Simple ordering through WhatsApp.

1

Browse

Browse the food menu above and choose your meal.

2

Choose a Store

Select Ndayini, Sivana or Birch Acres from the store buttons above.

3

Send & Confirm

Your order goes to the selected store’s WhatsApp. The store confirms availability, payment instructions and collection time. We do not offer delivery.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

Payment after order confirmation

Send your order to your selected store through WhatsApp. The store will confirm availability, the total and collection arrangements before sharing payment instructions. Online payments through Paystack are being prepared and will be available after activation.

For your protection, only use payment instructions supplied through Feasterville's official ordering process. Never share card PINs, passwords or one-time passwords with staff.
